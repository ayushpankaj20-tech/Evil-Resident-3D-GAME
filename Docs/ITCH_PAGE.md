## Description
**A creature trapped in a village has woken up — and the dead rise with it.**

Evil Resident is a third-person survival shooter. Fight your way through a haunted village, a hamlet full of
shield skeletons and bone throwers, and an arena where two bosses wait for you.

**Features**
- 3 stages and 2 bosses: THE CREATURE KNIGHT and THE REAL CREATURE (they talk, summon the dead and get enraged)
- Story intro, checkpoints, and a pause menu that always shows your objective
- Special enemies: crawlers that jump out of the ground, exploders that guard every key, gunmen on the roofs
- Easy (for kids) and Hard (recommended) difficulty
- Night and Day themes — beat the game at night to unlock day

**Controls**
- W A S D / arrows – move · Shift – run · Mouse – look
- Right mouse (hold) – aim · Left mouse – shoot · R – reload
- Esc or P – pause and objective
- In the browser: click the game to capture the mouse. Press P to pause (Esc only frees the mouse).
